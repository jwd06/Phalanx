Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by Phalanx. You can cancel at any time from your device's App Store account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions), or from the in-app subscription management screen. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not refund the current billing period.

How do I get a refund?

All payments are processed by Apple. We do not independently process refunds, and we cannot issue one on your behalf. Refund requests must be submitted directly to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, under Apple's refund policies.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the App, go to Profile → Account → Delete Account. The flow asks you to confirm twice. Deletion is immediate and permanent — your profile, every diary entry, your saved foods, and your food photos are erased, and there is no grace period and no recovery. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail of what is removed.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel the subscription separately in your Apple ID settings, or you may continue to be charged.

Why did my older food diary entries disappear?

This is by design. Food diary entries are automatically and permanently deleted on a rolling basis, generally within about five weeks of being created, and food photos are deleted along with the entries they belong to. Saved foods are not affected — they stay on your list until you remove them or delete your account.

Can I get a copy of my data?

We do not yet offer an automated export inside the App. Email us at the address above and we will put together a copy of your personal information and respond within a reasonable time.

Why does Phalanx ask for Apple Health access?

Only to read your step count, so your activity shows up alongside your nutrition data. Access is read-only — the App is configured so it cannot write anything back to Apple Health. Health access is optional, and you can revoke it at any time in your device's Health app settings.

Photo analysis or food search stopped working

Both features require a signed-in account, and both are rate limited to prevent abuse. If you have been scanning or searching rapidly, wait a few minutes and try again. If the problem continues, check that you are still signed in and that you have a working connection, then email us with the approximate time it happened.

What are the health condition settings for?

Under Profile → Nutrition → Health Conditions you can optionally indicate a diabetic or kidney condition. These answers are optional, are never asked for during onboarding, and are used only to adjust your macronutrient split. They are treated as sensitive health information — see the Privacy Policy.

What do I need to run Phalanx?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and an internet connection for food search, barcode lookup, and AI photo analysis.

I found a bug, or I want to request a feature

Email us. Bug reports with your full name and a description of what you were doing are the fastest to fix, and feature requests genuinely shape what gets built next.
