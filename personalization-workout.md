# Personalization Logic 

## Goal –> Split Type

| Goal | Recommended Split | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
|Lose weight | Full body | Compound full-body movements maximize calorie burn per session |
|Maintain Weight | Upper-Lower | Balanced volume and frequency |
|Gain weight | PPL | Maximum hypertrophy volume per muscle grp |

| Goal | Rep Range | Rest Period | Focus | 
|------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Lose Weight | 10-12 reps | 45-60s | Metabolic Conditioning |
| Maintain Weight | 8 - 12 reps | 60-90s | Balanced strength/hypertrophy |
| Gain Weight | 6 - 10 reps | 2-3 misn | Hypertrophy |
